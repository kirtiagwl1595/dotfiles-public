# Lesson style (Netflix Tech Blog)

Read by Claude before it writes a lesson to `.claude/lessons/` (pointer in `CLAUDE.md`, "Teach while working"). Kept out of CLAUDE.md so it loads only when a lesson is written.

- Byline: who did the work. Voice: "we" (or "I" for a solo story).
- Open with a concrete scene or a real number that shows why the problem matters, then one paragraph on what the post walks through.
- Headings tell the story ("Why not stop here?", "What was hard"), not generic labels.
- Show the journey: the first approach, where it broke, the options weighed, and when the chosen design is the wrong call.
- A diagram for each moving part. Real snippets, config, and before/after numbers.
- End with what we learned and what's next.
